Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data Security (No Cloud Storage)

ProTransfer operates on a Peer-to-Peer (P2P) model using WebRTC. We do NOT store, host, or save your files on our servers. Your data transfers directly from your device to the recipient's device.

2. End-to-End Encryption

All data channels are encrypted using DTLS/SRTP protocols. We cannot read, see, or intercept your files during the transfer process.

3. Personal Information

We do not collect any personal information for free transfers. Any data collected for analytics is anonymized.
